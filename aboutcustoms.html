About Customs Indore

Customs Indore is a key office under the Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC), Ministry of Finance, Government of India. Established as a full-fledged Commissionerate in 2002, it plays a critical role in facilitating legitimate trade while enforcing customs regulations across Madhya Pradesh and Chhattisgarh regions.

Vision

To be a world-class customs administration providing exceptional service and promoting compliance with customs laws to foster economic growth and security.

Mission

To facilitate trade and travel while enforcing laws to collect revenue, protect Indian society, and secure the international trade supply chain.

Formation and History

The Customs Commissionerate at Indore was established in 2002 as part of the restructuring of the Customs and Central Excise departments. Previously, the customs functions in the region were handled by the Central Excise Commissionerate.

The establishment of a dedicated Customs Commissionerate at Indore was necessitated by the growing international trade activities in the region, particularly after the development of Inland Container Depots (ICDs) and the expansion of Devi Ahilyabai Holkar Airport for international operations.

Over the years, the Commissionerate has evolved with technological advancements and policy reforms to meet the changing needs of international trade while maintaining effective customs control.
