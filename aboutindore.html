Highlights of Indore

Rajwada Palace

Built by the Holkars of the Maratha Empire, this seven-storey palace stands as a historical monument in the heart of the city, showcasing a blend of Maratha and Mughal architectural styles.

Lal Bagh Palace

One of the grandest monuments the Holkar dynasty left Indore, this palace reflects the taste, grandeur and lifestyle of the Holkars, the rulers of Indore.

Annapurna Temple

A beautiful temple dedicated to the goddess of food, Annapurna. The temple complex houses idols of various Hindu gods and goddesses and is known for its intricate architecture.

Indori Poha

Indore's signature breakfast dish, made with flattened rice flakes topped with sev, jalebi, and a unique mix of spices that you won't find anywhere else in India.

Sarafa Bazaar

A jewellery market by day that transforms into a street food paradise at night, offering a variety of local delicacies like bhutte ka kees, garadu, and more.

56 Dukan

Famous for its mouth-watering chaats and sweets, this food street is a must-visit for food lovers looking to experience authentic Indori flavors.

Patalpani Waterfall

A picturesque waterfall located 35km from Indore, it's a popular picnic spot especially during monsoon when the waterfall is in its full glory.

Central Museum

Housed in the Indore University, this museum displays a rich collection of prehistoric artifacts, sculptures, coins, and manuscripts that tell the story of Indore's history.

Gomatgiri

A sacred Jain pilgrimage site featuring a 21-foot statue of Lord Gomateshwara, similar to the one at Shravanabelagola in Karnataka.

IT & ITES Hub

Indore has emerged as a major IT and ITeS hub with numerous software parks and a skilled workforce, earning it the nickname 'Mini Mumbai'.

Crystal IT Park

A state-of-the-art IT park housing numerous tech companies and startups, contributing significantly to Indore's growing reputation as a tech destination.

Pithampur Industrial Area

One of Asia's largest industrial areas, home to numerous automotive, pharmaceutical, and textile manufacturing units, driving Indore's industrial growth.
