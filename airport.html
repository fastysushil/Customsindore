ATITHI@Indian Customs

ATITHI @Indian Customs is a mobile application by CBIC, Ministry of Finance, designed to simplify customs procedures for international travelers arriving in India. It enables quick filing of baggage, item, and currency declarations, reducing processing time at airports. The app is available on both Android and iOS platforms.

📌 Tips for a Smooth Customs Clearance

  • Always declare dutiable and restricted items at the Red Channel.
  • Keep receipts for high-value goods to avoid unnecessary delays.
  • Review the list of restricted items before packing.
  • Contact the customs helpdesk before travel for specific questions.
  • Use the Green Channel only if carrying duty-free permissible goods.
  • Use the ATITHI App for quick and easy customs declaration on arrival.
  • Request an Export Certificate for valuable personal items to carry them back without duty issues.
