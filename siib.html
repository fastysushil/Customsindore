📨 Submit Intelligence Tip

🔎 What You Can Report

  • Suspicious goods being exported or imported
  • Misdeclaration of quantity, value, or classification
  • Use of forged documents or identities in trade
  • Illegal currency or gold movement
  • Concealed contraband or narcotics
  • Over/under-invoicing in trade invoices
  • Smuggling activities around border and airport

💰 CBIC Reward Scheme for Informers

CBIC encourages responsible citizens to share credible information. Eligible informers may receive rewards based on the value of contraband, duty evasion, or recovered revenue.

  • Rewards up to 20% of net seizure value or duty recovered.
  • Identity of informer is kept strictly confidential.
  • Applies to seizures of contraband, duty evasion, or customs fraud.
  • Reward approval is tiered: Commissioner, Chief Commissioner, or Apex Committee depending on amount.
