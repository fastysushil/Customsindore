About Vigilance Awareness

Vigilance Awareness promotes transparency, integrity, and accountability in public service. At Customs Indore, we are committed to fostering a corruption-free environment through active vigilance and citizen participation.

Vigilance Awareness Week 2024

Vigilance Awareness Week 2024 was observed from 28th October 2024 to 3rd November 2024 on the designated theme.

Theme: " “सत्यनिष्ठा की संस्कृति से राष्ट्र की समृद्धि” “Culture of Integrity for Nation's Prosperity”"

Activities & Events

As part of the Vigilance Awareness, Customs Indore organizes various activities to promote integrity and transparency:

  • Workshops and seminars on anti-corruption measures
  • Distribution of pamphlets and display of banners
  • Integrity pledge ceremonies
  • Interactive sessions with stakeholders
